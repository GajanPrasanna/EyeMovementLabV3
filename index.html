Eye Movement Lab V3

Dual-device educational eye-tracking prototype

V3.0 prototype

Choose this device's role

Use the tablet for the target and report. Use the phone for the rear-camera eye view. Open this same website on both devices.

Educational prototype only. This software estimates a pupil-position signal from camera images. It is not a validated medical device and cannot diagnose cranial nerve palsy or an eye-muscle defect.

Tablet — Examination

Displays the target, receives measurements, runs calibration and shows the report for each eye.

Open tablet interface

Phone — Eye camera

Uses the phone camera, lets you select the eye region, visualises pupil tracking and sends measurements to the tablet.

Open phone camera interface

Recommended physical setup

Place the phone securely near the eye being examined, and place the tablet farther away in the participant's line of sight. Confirm that the camera can focus sharply before testing. Many phone cameras cannot focus at 5–10 cm; move the phone farther away if the eye is blurry. Keep the camera still and use even lighting.

This build uses image-processing to track a dark pupil-like region inside a manually selected eye region. It is a prototype signal, not validated gaze estimation.

Video stays in the phone browser in this source code. Only tracking values and examination events are sent to the tablet.
